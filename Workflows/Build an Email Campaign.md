# Build an Email Campaign

**Problem:** Create a complete email sequence from strategy through conversion.

**Outcome:** A full email campaign (5-10 emails) ready to deploy.

**Time investment:** 15-25 hours depending on complexity.

---

## Prerequisites

Before starting, you need:
- A clearly defined offer (product, price, mechanism, guarantee)
- A lead magnet or entry point (what got them on the list)
- Customer avatar and top objections
- Existing content or client wins you can pull stories from

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Campaign Strategy — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer/campaign-strategy|campaign-strategy]]

Define the entire campaign architecture before writing a single email.

**Deliverable:** Campaign Strategy Document with:
- Product overview (offer, price, mechanism, guarantee, bonuses)
- Lead magnet / entry point
- Customer avatar + awareness level
- Top 3 objections
- Life's 8 Triggers that apply to this offer
- Campaign type (Welcome Seq + BoFu or direct BoFu)
- Email count and timeline
- Campaign goal (# conversions, price point, revenue target)

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Questions you'll answer:**
- Who exactly is this for?
- What problem does it solve?
- What are they thinking right now?
- What do I need them to believe to buy?

**Decision point:** Does this strategy feel solid? Does it align with your offer?

---

### Step 2: Content Mining — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer/content-mining|content-mining]]

Extract the raw material you'll use in emails: stories, data, mechanisms, proof.

**Deliverable:** Content Mining Document with:
- Your origin story (how you got here)
- Your transformation story (before → after → how)
- 3-5 customer case studies (situation → action → result)
- Your mechanism in your own words (the "how" behind results)
- Personal vulnerabilities and failures (builds credibility)
- Data and research that supports your offer
- Email templates and proven openers

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**The raw material for this comes from:**
- Your past wins and clients
- Your own journey
- Conversations with customers
- Real DM exchanges and objections you've overcome

**Decision point:** Do you have enough genuine material to build a campaign on?

---

### Step 3: Email Brief Planner — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer/email-brief|email-brief]]

Outline each email before writing full copy.

**Deliverable:** Email Brief Document with:
- Sequence overview (all emails at a glance, 2-3 sentence summary each)
- Individual brief for each email in HPAGA.C format:
  - Hook (opens the email and frames the problem)
  - Problem (deepens why this matters)
  - Agitation (shows the cost of not solving it)
  - Gap (shows the gap between current state and desired state)
  - Argument (why this offer is the solution)
  - Call to action (what you want them to do)

**Time:** 3-5 hours
**Structure per email:** Each email should:
- Sell the next step (not the product directly)
- Build on the previous email's argument
- Include one story or data point
- Have a clear single CTA

**Decision point:** Does each email flow into the next? Would you want to receive these emails?

---

### Step 4: Email Writer — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer/email-writer|email-writer]]

Write the full copy for each email, one at a time.

**Deliverable:** Complete email sequence with final copy for all emails.

**Format per email:**
- Subject line (curiosity-driven or problem-driven)
- Preview text (what shows in inbox)
- Body copy (using the brief structure as skeleton)
- CTA link or button
- Preheader if needed for Obsidian linking

**Time:** 5-10 hours (longest step)
**Best practices included:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Conversational tone
- Stories and specificity over generic advice
- One clear CTA per email
- Consistency in voice with your brand

**Decision point:** Does each email sound like you? Would people want to read these?

---

## Execution Checklist

- [ ] Step 1: Campaign Strategy completed and approved
- [ ] Step 2: Content Mining done (stories, data, mechanism documented)
- [ ] Step 3: Email Brief complete (sequence and individual briefs)
- [ ] Step 4: All email copy written and proofread
- [ ] Subject lines tested for clarity and curiosity
- [ ] One person reads through the entire sequence start-to-finish
- [ ] Email platform configured (welcome sequence automation if needed)
- [ ] Tracking set up (open rates, click rates, conversions)
- [ ] Deployment scheduled or ready to send

## Email Sequence Archetype

**Welcome Sequence (optional, if you have a lead magnet):**
- Email 1: Welcome + deliver the lead magnet
- Email 2: Story about how you solved the problem for yourself

**Bottom-of-Funnel (Direct Sales):**
- Email 1: Credibility + curiosity hook
- Email 2: Problem deep-dive + story (objection #1)
- Email 3: Solution introduction + mechanism explanation
- Email 4: Social proof + case study (objection #2)
- Email 5: Urgency + scarcity frame
- Email 6: The offer (modules, bonuses, guarantee, price)
- Email 7: Objection handling (objection #3)
- Email 8: Last chance / deadline

Minimum viable sequence: 5 emails (Credibility → Problem → Story → Mechanism → Offer)

---

## Common Pitfalls

**Pitfall:** Jumping to writing without strategy.
**Fix:** Complete Step 1 first. The brief guides the writing.

**Pitfall:** Each email tries to sell the whole product.
**Fix:** Each email sells the next step. By email 6, they're ready to buy.

**Pitfall:** No stories. Pure logic and features.
**Fix:** Stories 2x conversion rate of logic alone. Mine content from your actual customers.

**Pitfall:** Campaign too long (15+ emails).
**Fix:** 5-8 emails is optimal. Longer sequences get lower open rates.

---

## When to Use This Workflow

- You have a new offer and need email sequence to launch it
- You're rebuilding a low-converting email campaign
- You want to test an offer with email before bigger marketing
- You're building a nurture/education sequence that eventually converts
