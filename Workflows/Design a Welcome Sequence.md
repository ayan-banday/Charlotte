# Design a Welcome Sequence

**Problem:** Set up onboarding emails for new leads before main sales sequence.

**Outcome:** A 3-5 email welcome sequence that builds authority and preps for the pitch.

**Time investment:** 5-10 hours.

**When to use:** Only if you're running a lead magnet (freebie in exchange for email). If no lead magnet, skip this and go straight to [[Build an Email Campaign]].

---

## Prerequisites

- A lead magnet or freebie (PDF, video, challenge, training, etc.)
- Your story of how you solved this problem
- Understanding of your customer's current awareness level

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Welcome Campaign Strategy — [[Campaign Strategy]]

Define the welcome sequence's job before writing.

**Key questions to answer:**
- What lead magnet are they getting?
- What's the timeline? (same day, daily, every other day?)
- How many emails? (3-5 is typical)
- What's the bridge from lead magnet to main offer?
- What beliefs do you need to install while they consume the lead magnet?

**Deliverable:** Mini-strategy for the welcome sequence (1-2 hours)

**Decision point:** Does this welcome sequence prep them to hear your offer?

---

### Step 2: Welcome Email Architecture — [[Welcome Sequence]]

Outline the complete welcome sequence before writing.

**Typical structure (3-5 emails):**

**Email 1 (Immediate delivery):**
- Welcome confirmation
- Deliver the lead magnet + how to access
- Set expectations for next emails
- Light credibility (you understand their problem)

**Email 2 (24 hours later):**
- Tell the story of how you solved this (vulnerability + transformation)
- Mention the mechanism briefly (not the offer yet)
- Build curiosity for what's coming

**Email 3 (Day 3 or 4):**
- Deeper dive into the mechanism (how it works)
- Case study or example of it working
- Start positioning your offer as the accelerator

**Email 4 (Day 5-7, optional):**
- Remove a major objection
- Show what's possible (aspirational content)
- Hint at the main offer coming

**Email 5 (Day 7-10, optional):**
- Transition email into main sales sequence
- Recap what they've learned
- Introduce the offer (or preview it)

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Decision point:** Does this sequence naturally flow into your main offer?

---

### Step 3: Welcome Email Copy — [[Welcome Sequence]]

Write the actual email copy for each email.

**For each email:**
- Subject line that matches the email's purpose
- Body copy (conversational, 150-300 words typical)
- Single clear CTA
- Consistent signature/voice

**Format guidance:**
- Email 1: Transactional but warm (welcome + delivery)
- Email 2: Story-driven (your transformation)
- Email 3: Mechanism explanation with proof
- Email 4-5: Bridge to main offer

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Decision point:** Would you want to receive these emails?

## Welcome Sequence Archetypes

### Short Welcome (3 emails)
1. Welcome + deliver lead magnet
2. Your story (how you solved it)
3. Bridge to main offer

**Best for:** Simple offers, short sales cycle, time-sensitive campaigns

### Full Welcome (5 emails)
1. Welcome + lead magnet delivery
2. Your transformation story
3. Mechanism explanation
4. Objection removal + case study
5. Bridge to main offer

**Best for:** Complex offers, skeptical audiences, high-ticket items

### Minimal Welcome (2 emails)
1. Welcome + lead magnet delivery
2. Brief story + transition to main sequence

**Best for:** Warm audiences, repeat buyers, time-constrained launches

---

## Common Pitfalls

**Pitfall:** Welcome sequence is too salesy too fast.
**Fix:** First 2 emails should deliver value, not push the offer. Let them settle in.

**Pitfall:** No story. Just delivering the lead magnet and jumping to the offer.
**Fix:** Your story is the bridge. It shows proof without claiming it.

**Pitfall:** Welcome sequence too long (7+ emails).
**Fix:** 3-5 emails max. After that, they're ready for your main sales sequence.

**Pitfall:** Timing is off (too frequent or too slow).
**Fix:** Daily is best for engagement. Every other day is acceptable. Slower than that loses momentum.

## Connection to Main Campaign

The welcome sequence feeds directly into [[Build an Email Campaign]].

**Handoff point:** Last email of welcome sequence transitions readers into the first email of your main sales campaign.

**Timing:** Welcome sequence runs first (7-10 days), then main campaign takes over.
