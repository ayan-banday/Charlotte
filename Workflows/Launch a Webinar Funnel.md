# Launch a Webinar Funnel

**Problem:** Build a complete webinar-based offer machine from strategy through close.

**Outcome:** A fully scripted, conversion-optimized webinar ready to run.

**Time investment:** 10-20 hours depending on complexity and experience.

---

## Prerequisites

Before starting this workflow, you need:
- A clearly defined offer (product name, price, core mechanism)
- Target audience avatar
- Top 3 objections you know people have
- Initial content ideas or existing case studies

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Strategic Foundation — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Webinar Architecture/webinar-planning|Strategy & Structure]]

Define the entire webinar architecture before writing a single slide.

**Deliverable:** Completed strategy document with:
- Offer definition and tagline
- Target audience + explicit exclusions
- 3 core objections = your 3 content sections
- Hook angle (the big promise)
- Price anchoring framework
- Guarantee and bonus structure

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Decision point:** Does this strategy feel tight? Do you want to adjust before moving forward?

---

### Step 2: Opening Hook — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Webinar Architecture/webinar-hook|Opening Hook]]

Write the first 5-15 minutes that decide if people stay or leave.

**Deliverable:** Complete opening slides with word-for-word copy.

**Key elements included:**
- Audience segmentation (who this is for, who it isn't)
- Problem statement + empathy
- Credibility stacking
- Hope statement
- Transition into content

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Decision point:** Does this opening make you want to listen? Would you stay for the rest?

---

### Step 3: Intro Builder — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Webinar Architecture/webinar-intro|Intro Builder]]

Build the full intro phase with all commitment devices in place.

**Deliverable:** Complete intro slides with structure for credentials, social proof, and commitment hooks.

**What gets added:**
- Full credibility framework (credentials, authority, social proof)
- Commitment devices (polls, questions, yes-ladder)
- Dives into the mechanism
- Transition to content promise

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Decision point:** After this intro, do listeners understand your credentials and feel committed to listening?

---

### Step 4: Content Builder — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Webinar Architecture/webinar-value|Content Builder]]

Build the 3 teaching sections that destroy objections and build buying momentum.

**Deliverable:** Complete content architecture for all 3 sections with teaching frameworks.

**Structure per section (12-20 min each):**
- Reframe the objection
- Teach the counterintuitive insight
- Show the mechanism
- Give them the framework to use it
- Small yes-gathering questions throughout

**Time:** 4-6 hours (most time-intensive step)
**Decision point:** By the end of content, does the listener think "I need help with this" instead of "I can do this myself"?

---

### Step 5: Transition Bridge — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Webinar Architecture/webinar-transition|Transition Bridge]]

Create the emotional and logical bridge from content to pitch.

**Deliverable:** 3-5 minute transition sequence with:
- 60-second recap of what you taught
- "Yes-street" (series of yes questions)
- 2-option frame (This or nothing? My way or struggle?)
- Warm transition into the pitch

**Time:** 1-2 hours
**Decision point:** Does this transition feel natural and set up the pitch without pressure?

---

### Step 6: Pitch & Close — [[Skills Library/Marketing/Webinar Architecture/webinar-close|Pitch & Close]]

Present the offer, bonuses, price, and guarantee with full framework.

**Deliverable:** Complete pitch slides with:
- Module breakdown (what's included)
- Price anchoring (higher anchors, your price)
- Bonuses tied to objections
- Guarantee/risk reversal
- Scarcity/urgency (seat limits, deadline)
- Clear call to action + URL

**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Decision point:** Does the offer feel premium, valuable, and achievable to the right person?

---

## Execution Checklist

- [ ] Step 1 complete and approved
- [ ] Step 2 complete and practiced (can you deliver it naturally?)
- [ ] Step 3 complete and polished
- [ ] Step 4 complete (test teach it to someone if possible)
- [ ] Step 5 written and flows naturally
- [ ] Step 6 complete with all pricing/bonus/guarantee elements
- [ ] Full webinar read through at normal pace (should be 90-120 min total)
- [ ] Slides designed and branded
- [ ] Webinar platform set up (Zoom, etc.)
- [ ] Funnel page + email sequence built to drive registrations

---

## Common Pitfalls

**Pitfall:** Starting with the intro. Skips strategy definition.
**Fix:** Always do Step 1 first. Every webinar slides is there to serve the pitch.

**Pitfall:** Content too long. Runs out of time for pitch.
**Fix:** Cap content at 50-60 min. Pitch needs 30-45 min minimum.

**Pitfall:** Pitch feels like a hard sell after good teaching.
**Fix:** Use transition bridge properly. Make the offer feel like a logical next step, not a pivot.

---

## When to Use This Workflow

- You have a new offer and need a webinar to launch it
- You want to test an offer with a webinar before bigger marketing
- Your current webinar conversion is low and needs rebuilding from scratch
