## 6. The Examiner's Mental Model

**There is no examiner.** `[SOURCED]` What replaces one, from the student's objective:

- **The system is not arbitrary, and treating it as arbitrary is the failure mode.** Every rule in
  this workbook descends from one identity: **Assets = Liabilities + Equity**, kept true by every
  transaction. `[INFERRED — the workbook never states the equation; see topic_guide.md §4]`
- **The named benchmark is rote-trained.** Students drilled on golden rules can journalise fluently
  and still not say what a debit *is*. The edge being pursued is derivation, not speed.
- **Bookkeeping is the floor, not the ceiling.** The course is named *Accounting for Business
  **Decisions***; the workbook only reaches the trial balance. That gap is real and it is out of
  scope by the student's decision `[SOURCED]` — recorded, not silently closed.
