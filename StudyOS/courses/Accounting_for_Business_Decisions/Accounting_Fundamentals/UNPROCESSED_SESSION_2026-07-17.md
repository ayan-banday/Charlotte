## Candidate Priority 1 — capital vs profit

**The student said, three times, with no hedge:**

> *"Capital 20 lakh — **it's money that we made**."*

> *"What we have is, I guess, **the capital plus the net profit**."*

> *"…and the capital itself is gonna be **the money we made**, the source of things."*

**Capital is what the owner PUT IN. Profit is what the business MADE.** They are two different
origins, and the balance sheet prints them on two separate lines precisely because they are not the
same thing:

```
   Capital          20,00,000   ← the owner supplied this on day 1
   Add: Net Profit     10,000   ← the business earned this by trading
   ─────────────────────────
   Equity           20,10,000
```

**Why this is a candidate P1, not a slip:**

- Stated **three separate times** across seven minutes.
- **No hesitation marker on any of them** — no "I think", no "I guess", no self-correction. Contrast
  with everything else in this session, which is soaked in hedges.
- It is **adjacent to the P1 already logged** (`sources_vs_uses`, C8). The student corrected
  liabilities-as-uses, then mis-stated capital-as-profit **forty minutes later**. The same
  sources/uses confusion, wearing different clothing.

**Node:** C6 (expanded equation) / A5 (equity & capital) — currently both marked UNTOUCHED.

**Suggested test if processed:** two businesses close the month with identical equity of
Rs 20,10,000. In one, the owner put in Rs 20,10,000 and the business broke even. In the other, the
owner put in Rs 20,00,000 and the business earned Rs 10,000. Same equity, same balance sheet total.
