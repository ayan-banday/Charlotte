### C. The equation — the spine `[EXT — INFERRED throughout; see §4]`
> **The workbook never states the accounting equation.** It teaches Dual Aspect (B5) and golden rules
> (D4–D6) as separate, unjustified givens. This section is the derivation the student asked for. It is
> `[INFERRED]` in the strict traceability sense — but it is not speculation: it is the standard
> derivation, checkable, and it is what makes B5 and D4–D6 stop being arbitrary.
- [ ] **C1** **Assets = Liabilities + Equity** — every transaction must keep it true
- [ ] **C2** Dual Aspect (B5) *is* this identity — the workbook's "two-fold aspect" restated
- [ ] **C3** Debit and credit as **directions**, not meanings — left/right of the equation
- [ ] **C4** Why assets and expenses share a normal debit balance; why liabilities, equity and revenue share a credit
- [ ] **C5** Deriving each golden rule from C1 — the rules as *shortcut*, not foundation
- [ ] **C6** The expanded equation — Equity = Capital + Revenue − Expenses − Drawings
- [ ] **C7** Translating both ways — equation ↔ golden-rule vocabulary (the benchmark's language)
- [ ] **C8** **Sources vs uses** — the left side says *what we have*, the right says *where it came from*. Same rupees, described twice. **This is why the sheet balances at all.** `[P1 GAP — student inverted this 2026-07-17: "liabilities... the things we've spent the money on"]`
- [ ] **C9** **Asset vs expense boundary** — the test is *do you still control a resource afterward?* Machinery = asset swap, equity unmoved. Rent = consumed, equity down. Downstream of C8.
- [ ] **C10** What is forced vs what is arbitrary — *which side an account lives on* is forced by the equation; *calling the left column "debit"* is arbitrary naming. Conflating them causes memorization of derivable facts.

## 3. Mark Scheme Logic (this topic specifically)

No rubric exists (`context.md` §3). The standard, from the student's objective `[SOURCED]`:

**Earns credit**
- Deriving a rule from **A = L + E** rather than citing it
- Correct treatment with the **reason** stated — "cash comes in, so debit" beats a bare entry
- Recognizing that an event triggers **no entry** at all
- Naming the *specific* concept in play, not "it's a concept"

**Loses credit**
- Golden rules recited with nothing under them (the benchmark's failure mode — `course_guide.md` §6)
- A right entry with wrong reasoning → **treated as wrong.** The objective is first-principles
  understanding; a correct answer reached by pattern-match is exactly the delta zone `spec.md` §4
  targets, and defaults to FRAGILE at best
- Booking a credit purchase to Cash
- Treating a tallied trial balance as proof of correctness

**Type is `quantitative`** — reading never closes a gap here (`spec.md` §2). Five variants, zero
hesitation, correct method each time, or it stays open. Concept nodes (§B) inherit this type and close
by application to fresh scenarios, not restatement — the friction is recorded in `context.md` §4.
