**sources_vs_uses** — node C8
State: ACTIVE   ·   Priority: **1**
Session 1 (2026-07-17): **WRONG, confident** — unprompted self-report, no hedging language present.
Student's exact words: *"liabilities, which is just the things we've spent the money on."*
The misconception: liabilities read as **uses** of money (things bought) instead of **sources**
(claims by outsiders). Inverts the right-hand side of A = L + E. Under this model the equation
double-counts — assets and liabilities become the same rupees — and cannot balance.

error was stated as settled understanding.

correction was given in-session on 2026-07-17 and the student did not push back — that is *not*
evidence of closure. Requires 5 variants solved, zero hesitation, correct method each time.

State: ACTIVE   ·   Priority: 3
