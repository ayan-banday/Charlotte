Most people will die having lived a life they never truly wanted.

You've been handed a script.

And the tragedy is that you've been following it to-the-dot.

Start a business.

Reach KPI for scale metrics.

Hire more people, automate most work.

Live off of 2 hours of work per day.

You leveled up by quitting the 9-5' retire at 65, but you got stuck in a golden prison.

Do you really want just money?

I will never forget the first month I made $500. I was 17 and excited to buy a fancy carbon-fiber electrum digital guitar that I could connect to my laptop. It could change the sound it made.

I ordered it off of Amazon. I couldn't control the tingles in my legs, jumping around the house thinking about it reaching me. And then it came.

I played it, closed my eyes, and let the strings reverberate the accomplishment of having delivered value. I live in India, and $500 is a lot of money. To have done it, I felt I made it.

That was my first taste of freedom, to not ask permission from my parents to buy something.

And as Naval says:

> A taste of freedom makes you unemployable.

The next day I realized how stupid of a decision that was. I picked up the guitar and its wires, put them in the same box it came in, and returned it. Seven days later I had my refund.

It was a hard choice to make, to commit myself to being free. I had tasted freedom and couldn't go back. From that day on, every decision led me closer to being freer.

As things go in business, things got harder. I could freelance and make money that way, but business is an entirely different game. I was scared to reach out to prospects.

I would record a one-minute personalized selfie video and send ten of them to people on LinkedIn.

Over that week I got rejected more times than I know people in the entire world. After a few hundred prospects and a few VSLs, I finally had a person on the call.

It went as you would expect.

I started out by saying, "In this call we're going to figure out if we can work together." He asked, "What do you do?" I replied, "I want to understand first if I could help you, then I'd let you know. How can I help you?"

During the call I prompted him to tell me about his business. How he was expanding to the USA. How his business was actually doing. It was intrusive. I went in there with a katana to cut through his business and maybe thought I'd be his massaiah. The response was as intended.

He got fucking furious. I could see the veins in his forehead pop up for battle. I told him, "Based on this conversation, I don't think I can help you." He hung up. 3 minutes later he called me on my phone number. I didn't pick up, Blocked him after that.

He messaged me twice on WhatsApp and I blocked him there. I felt my body shrink into a dried resin.

I came running downstairs, and my gut felt like Mike Tyson was warming up for a championship. I'd never felt anything like it in my life. I picked up my bicycle and rode out to let the cortisol burn through my body.

The price of freedom is paid in putting a dagger through your ego and cutting off the parts that don't align with the vision.

It means burning dead wood. And that fucking sucks.

People try to game the pain. Pick an easier path. Learn from a mentor. Buy a course. But you cannot escape the pain- not fully. Worst of all, you cannot let yourself stray away from the path.

This newsletter is for the kind of person who knows they don't just want money. They want a happy life, relationships, time spent doing what they love. And they have realized they are afraid to take the action that would actually get them there.

I want to help you close the gap between the life you have right now and the life you actually wanted when you started a business. Specifically, I want to show you exactly what made you choose business, how you forgot about those reasons, and how you're still stuck in the same script you tried to burn.
