# Example VSL Transcript — Coaching/Creator Licensing Program

This is a real-world high-converting VSL for a coaching business licensing program. Use this as the structural blueprint. The VSL follows a specific sequence of sections that build on each other. Every section has a job — if it doesn't move the viewer closer to the CTA, it doesn't belong.

---

## Section 1: Pattern Interrupt / Anti-Polish Open

The VSL opens casual and raw — explicitly saying "didn't have the patience for fancy presentations, so I just cooked up a quick Google Doc." This is intentional. It signals authenticity, breaks the pattern of polished sales videos, and creates the feeling of getting insider access rather than being marketed to.

**Key elements:**
- Casual, unpolished tone ("honestly, didn't have patience...")
- Positions as insider access, not a sales pitch
- States the core offer immediately: licensing the actual business operating system
- Differentiates hard from courses/coaching: "Not another course, not another coaching program — the actual SOPs, scripts, automations, and systems"
- Drops a credibility anchor early: "running 200k a month businesses right now"
- Creates scarcity: "small group", "a few more licensing spots over the next seven weeks"

---

## Section 2: Origin Story + Credibility Stack

Transitions from the open into the creator's backstory — but only the parts that build credibility for the specific offer. Not a life story. A proof stack.

**Key elements:**
- Age-anchored achievements: "When I was 22, I built a 100k/month agency. By 24, a 100k/month software/coaching company."
- Wasn't teaching — was doing: "I wasn't teaching anyone anything. I was just in the trenches doing the work."
- Content blew up accidentally: "What started sharing a few tips turned into a content machine"
- Specific numbers: 75M+ views, 12 videos crossing 1M views, 643,000 comments
- DMs exploded → pattern recognition: hundreds of thousands of messages, all asking the same thing
- Transition to empathy: "These were talented coaches... real expertise, but they were struggling"

---

## Section 3: Social Proof Wave 1 — Transformation Stories

Immediately after establishing empathy for the audience's struggle, the VSL drops concrete transformation stories. These aren't vague. They're specific people with specific numbers and emotional context.

**Key elements:**
- Gabe: asking parents for $20 for McDonald's → $80,000 profit/month, parents finally said they're proud
- Paul: 37, married, made £30,000 ($39,500) in sales in ONE day
- Daniel: doomscrolling with zero income at 21 → paid off mom's mortgage, hit $96,000 halfway through the month

**Pattern:** Each story follows: [Name] + [relatable painful "before"] → [specific dollar result] + [emotional payoff]

---

## Section 4: The Differentiator — "I'm Not Selling Education, I'm Licensing Infrastructure"

This is the positioning pivot. The VSL explicitly separates itself from every other program.

**Key elements:**
- "I'm not selling you education. I'm licensing you my infrastructure."
- "Every SOP, every script, every automation, every system running 200k/month businesses right now — yours to install and use."
- "Most programs give you frameworks and tell you to figure it out. I'm giving you the actual plug and play assets."
- Speed promise: "On or before the first 16 to 90 days, you should expect to be enrolling high ticket clients consistently."
- Credibility anchor: "I've been at this for seven years, so there's no problem we won't be able to solve."

---

## Section 5: Social Proof Wave 2 — Speed of Results

More testimonials, but now focused on speed — how fast results came.

**Key elements:**
- Jermaine: $4,000 upfront first week, contracted $8,500, then moved to Bali
- Renee: first $12,500 client within 4 days
- Omish: $1,000/month client within 24 hours

**Pattern:** Speed + specific dollar amounts. "Within [timeframe]" is repeated.

---

## Section 6: The Unique Mechanism — "The Three Jobs Trap"

This is the intellectual core of the VSL. A named framework that reframes the audience's problem in a way they haven't heard before. This is what makes the viewer think "oh shit, that's exactly what's happening to me."

**Key elements:**
- Named concept: "The Three Jobs Trap"
- "When you started your coaching business, you thought you were starting one business. But you accidentally started three completely different jobs."
- Job 1: The Coach (zone of genius — not the problem)
- Job 2: The Creator (content — exhausting but doing it)
- Job 3: The Closer (converting followers to clients — where everything breaks)
- "Most coaches hate selling. So what do you do instead? You post more content."
- Brutal truth: "If nobody is buying, nobody is being helped."
- Pizza maker analogy: "You're like the best pizza maker in town who refuses to take orders."
- Proof via Anthony: not great coach, but landing clients because he has systems for Job 3 ($12,000/month)

---

## Section 7: The Solution Framework — "The Enrollment Ecosystem"

After naming the problem (Three Jobs Trap), the VSL introduces the solution — also as a named system.

**Key elements:**
- "The solution isn't to get better at sales... The solution is to install a system that does the selling for you."
- Steve Jobs / iPhone analogy: "He walked on stage, revealed what he built, and people lined up around the block... Apple didn't convince people to buy. Apple made buying feel inevitable."
- Named system: "The Enrollment Ecosystem"
- "Your positioning does the selling, your content does the selling, your offer structure does the selling, your follow-up does the selling."
- "It's like having an invisible salesperson working 24/7 while you focus on what you're actually great at. Coaching."

---

## Section 8: Social Proof Wave 3 — Lifestyle + Income

More testimonials, now showing lifestyle transformation alongside income.

**Key elements:**
- Yassine: 8 months watching YouTube with zero clients → $35,000/month profit, travels whenever
- Victoria: hit $12,000 months
- Megan: stuck at $10k feeling like a sitting duck → $30k months, husband quitting army

---

## Section 9: Universality Proof — "It Works for Everyone"

Handles the objection "but will it work for ME?" by showing it works across demographics and niches.

**Key elements:**
- Age range: Levin (16 years old, $17,000 client), Braden (14), Larry (almost 50, $2,000 first week)
- "It doesn't matter what niche you're in" — lists health, business, relationship, mindset, creative coaches
- "The framework is universal. You bring the what (your expertise). I bring the how (the plug and play systems)."

---

## Section 10: Competition Reframe — "Why Other Programs Failed You"

Directly addresses why the viewer's past purchases didn't work — and positions this offer as the fix.

**Key elements:**
- "Most programs teach more content strategies, more posting, more platform hacks. They make Job 2 even bigger while completely ignoring Job 3."
- "That's why those 5k to 20k programs you bought didn't work. They were solving the wrong problem."
- "You're licensing the exact operating system behind 200k/month businesses. Not theory, not frameworks — the actual SOPs, scripts, automations, and assets."
- Proof: Roe restarted from zero → $13,500 collected in a couple weeks, only works 3 hours/day

---

## Section 11: The Offer Stack

Detailed breakdown of everything included. Each item is numbered and named.

**Key elements:**
1. Cash Injection Week — revenue in first 7 days
2. AI Foundation — build entire offer with AI in days instead of weeks
3. Signature Offer — course architecture and packaging system
4. Enrollment Ecosystem Setup — AI VSL builder, funnels, automations, AI setters, lead scoring
5. Content Engine — content system that turns followers into clients, less posting
6. DM Playbook — turn DMs into #1 source of high ticket clients
7. Closures Playbook — word-for-word Nine Box sales script + objection handling + call film room

**Bonuses:**
1. Inner Game — personal development system, confidence, identity, daily routines
2. Copy Edge — core copywriting fundamentals
3. Breaking Down Pricing Limiting Beliefs — overcome mental blocks on charging
4. LLC/S-Corp Setup + Financial Tracker

---

## Section 12: Access + Community Details

Addresses the "how does this actually work" question.

**Key elements:**
- Private member's platform with all SOPs, scripts, automations, trainings
- Go at your own pace
- Private community (10k to $100k+/month licensees)
- Twice weekly live calls with personal Q&A, offer review, obstacle removal
- "If something isn't working, we fix it together."

---

## Section 13: Price Anchoring + Investment

**Key elements:**
- Future price anchor: "$16,000 when I open to the public"
- Honest framing: "I'm not gonna sugarcoat this. This isn't cheap."
- Early bird positioning: "Because you're getting in early, if you agree to give me an honest testimonial..."
- Defers exact pricing to the roadmap call: "My team will give you guidance on which option is best"

---

## Section 14: The Guarantee — Risk Reversal

This is aggressive and specific.

**Key elements:**
- "If you implement 80% of my plan and don't enroll at least 20 high ticket clients in 180 days, I'll give you a full refund of everything you paid, PLUS I'll wire you $5,000."
- Repeats for emphasis: "Not just a refund — a full refund, and I'll send you $5,000 on top of it."
- Confidence signal: "I'm so confident this works that I'll literally pay you if it doesn't work."

---

## Section 15: Best Case / Worst Case Close

Future pacing that makes both outcomes feel safe.

**Best case:**
- Success story like top students
- Enrolling high ticket clients consistently
- 20-30 hours/week instead of 50-60
- Freedom: vacations, present for family
- Kyle: took girlfriend to Fort Lauderdale, made $60k that month
- First 10k, then 20k, then 50k, then 100k/month
- "Beaming with confidence because you're finally living the life you imagined"

**Worst case:**
- "Maybe it doesn't work for you" (acknowledges reality)
- Guarantee kicks in: show 80% effort for 180 days → full refund + $5,000
- "Completely risk free"

**CTA:**
- "Apply below" → book free one-on-one roadmap call
- "I'll see you on the other side."
