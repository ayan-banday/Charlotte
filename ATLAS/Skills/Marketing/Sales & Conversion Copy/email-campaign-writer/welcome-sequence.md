# Prompt 0 — Welcome Sequence

Load Skills Library/Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer/email-campaign-writer.md → Optional Welcome Sequence section.

I want to set up a welcome sequence before my sales emails. Walk me through the welcome emails.

Ask me:
- Did they download/see/access a specific lead magnet? What was it?
- What promise was made to them?
- What's your transformation story? (The "before and after" that matters most)
- What's your mechanism — why does what you teach actually work?
- How many welcome emails do you want? (Usually 2–3)

One question at a time.

At the end: complete welcome email briefs using the HPAGA C framework from Phase 2. I'll approve each one before we move to the sales sequence.

Remember: Welcome emails install the beliefs that make the sales sequence land.
