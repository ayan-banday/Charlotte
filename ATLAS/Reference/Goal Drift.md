# Goal Drift

## Problem Solved
Goal drift happens when you fixate so intensely on a proxy metric or intermediate target that you lose sight of the actual big-picture goal. You end up optimizing for the wrong thing.

**Real-world example:** Someone wants to get into college. They start preparing for the entrance exam. They go all in. They get rank 1 in the entire country. But somewhere in that obsession, they forgot that getting into college requires money. They have no funding. They fuck up.

This is everywhere. You're chasing the visible target so hard you miss what actually matters.

## Key
Whenever you're thinking of any goal, pull up the big picture goal. Ask: what actually needs to happen for the big picture goal? Not just the proxy.

Also consider: important vs urgent tasks. Don't let urgency steal from importance.

Define the lock before you carve the tiny puny key.
