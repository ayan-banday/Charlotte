# Newsletter Do Hard Things — Project Introduction

**One-sentence description:** A newsletter for high-performers who've misunderstood hard work, teaching them that the goal isn't easier life but loving the climb itself.

---

## Why This Project Exists

**Connected to RGS:** Audience expansion. Building a voice in the high-performer space.

**Problem it solves:** High performers think hard work leads to easier life. It doesn't. When they reach the top they collapse because they're chasing rest, not meaning. The newsletter teaches the reframe: upgrade your problems, not eliminate them. Fall in love with the work.

**Success looks like:** Audience that understands "hard things" as the goal, not the price. They're not seeking rest. They're seeking better problems only they can solve.

---

## Current Status

**Phase:** Ideation and positioning (Week 1).

**What's been done:** Core spine established. Three foundational ideas anchored: Identity Over Effort, Create Your Own Problems, Choose Your Hard Problems.

**What's in progress:** Developing the writer's voice. Building the deeper mechanics around how identity shifts enable hard work.

**What's blocked:** None yet.

**Next milestone:** First draft of opening issue (ICP positioning + hook)
