# Newsletter Do Hard Things — Overview

**Project:** Newsletter positioning high performers toward meaningful work over comfort.

**Goal:** Build a newsletter that teaches the reframe: hard work upgrades your problems, it doesn't eliminate them. The goal is learning to love the climb.

**Connected to:** Audience expansion and voice development in high-performer space.

---

## Core Spine (The Newsletter's Foundation)

1. **Identity Over Effort** — Being precedes doing. Identity shifts create different actions and intentions.
2. **Upgrade Problems, Don't Solve Them** — The work doesn't get lighter. It gets more valuable and more specific to you.
3. **Choose Your Hard Problems** — Specialization in the problems only you can solve makes you unstoppable.

## Key Decisions & Assumptions

- **ICP:** High performers (founders, builders, ambitious creators) age 25-45
- **Core assumption:** This audience has been taught that hard work = easier life. They're frustrated when reaching goals doesn't deliver rest.
- **Reframe:** The goal isn't rest. The goal is becoming someone who can handle bigger, better problems.
