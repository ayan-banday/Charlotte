# Webinar Funnel: Procrastination

Build a complete, conversion-optimized webinar funnel for procrastination solution targeting ICS students.

**Core Insight:**
ICS students are not procrastinating in the classical sense. They're studying the wrong thing and calling it progress. They redirect energy toward meta-system optimization (rebuilding schedules, reorganizing mindmaps, researching learning techniques) instead of actual retrieval practice. This feels productive because it IS work. But it's the one type of work that can never fail.

Central reframe: "You're already working. You're just working on the one thing that can never fail."

**Audience:**
- For: ICS students actively using techniques but not getting results in their desired timeframe, with effort that feels disproportionate to output
- Not for: Students already getting results with ICS in their desired timeframe and effort level

**Workflow Structure:** 4 phases
1. Phase 1: Webinar Design (strategy & architecture)
2. Phase 2: Email Campaign (registration sequence)
3. Phase 3: Webinar Delivery (slides, platform, automation)
4. Phase 4: Post-Webinar Sequence (close sequence)

**Key Skills Used:**
- `webinar-planning` — Phase 1 webinar strategy
- `campaign-strategy`, `content-mining`, `email-brief`, `email-writer` — Phase 2 emails
- `webinar-hook`, `webinar-value`, `webinar-transition`, `webinar-close` — Phase 3 refinement
- Post-webinar email system — Phase 4

**Speaker:** Core member of ICS community (top 5 of 10-15 core members). Years of experience. Peer positioning but with authority.

**Status:** Phase 1 in progress (webinar design)
