## 2. Proposed learning methodology

Run this loop for each course alongside the syllabus:

1. **Name bottlenecks:** Identify the skill gaps for the semester.
2. **Gather inputs:** Map learning outcomes and assigned material to those gaps.
3. **Learn and apply:** Study independently and apply concepts directly to Udyaan or another live project where relevant.
4. **Present update:** Every one to two weeks, check in with the relevant teacher, covering roughly two courses at a time, by presentation or structured email.
5. **Get feedback:** Use faculty feedback to identify the gaps.
6. **Close gaps:** Revise and present in the next cycle, or move on when the gap is closed.

Skill gaps run alongside academic requirements, not instead of them. Assessments follow the same rhythm and remain aligned with what the rest of the cohort is covering.

## 4. Applied work: Udyaan

Udyaan is the university project I have been assigned to direct. I document the work as I go: problem definition, research, and packaging development. Where coursework overlaps, I present the problem solved, how it connects to the course, and which learning outcome it demonstrates.

## 5. Example: finance loop

This loop is already running for managerial accounting and finance.

| Stage | Activity |
|---|---|
| Week 0 | Talk to the teacher, get outcomes and books. |
| Week 1: study | Work through Chapters 1 to 3 and connect pricing to Udyaan. |
| Week 1: feedback | Teacher identifies margin and assumption gaps. |
| Week 2: revise | Fix gaps and present revised work. |
| Week 2+ | Start new material, or go deeper where gaps remain. |

**Books in use:** Assigned course texts, plus supplementary material agreed with the teacher.

**Cadence:** A presentation every one to two weeks, with the revision presented in the cycle that follows.
