# Prompt 0.5 — Content Mining

Load Skills Library/Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer/email-campaign-writer.md → Phase 1 (look at the Personal Input section).

I want to extract the raw material from my own experience that will power the email campaign.

Ask me about:
- Your origin story: How did you get here? What was broken about your situation?
- Your transformation: What was the turning point? What changed?
- 3–5 customer case studies: Specific people, specific results, specific timeframes
- Your mechanism explained in your own words: How does what you teach actually work?
- Your vulnerabilities and failures: What didn't work before you figured it out?
- Data and research that supports your offer: Numbers, studies, proof
- Real DM exchanges: Exact quotes from customers about their problems and objections

One question at a time. Go deep. Raw material only — we'll structure it in the strategy phase.

At the end: complete Content Mining Document with all the raw stories, quotes, and proof points that will feed into the email briefs.

This is the foundation. Better raw material = better emails.
