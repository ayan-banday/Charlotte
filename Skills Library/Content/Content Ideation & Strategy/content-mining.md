# Prompt 0.5 — Content Mining

Runs once before Phase 1. Collects the raw material from which all emails are built.

Without this step, the emails remain generic — with it, they'll sound like you.

--

Load Marketing/Sales & Conversion Copy/email-campaign-writer.md.

I want to collect raw material for an email campaign about: [TOPIC / PRODUCT]

**IMPORTANT:** Copy all answers verbatim — no paraphrasing, no "improvements." Jani usually records using SuperWhisper. His exact words are the raw material from which the emails will later sound like him. Structuring is allowed. Do not change the words.

Ask me in order — one question at a time, never all at once:

**Block 1 — Your Stories**

- What was your personal low point with this topic? (Specific scene — when, where, what happened)

- What was the moment when something changed? (Epiphany — what did you realize?)

- What decision did you make that changed everything?

- Is there a moment when you realized "it works"? (Specifically — number, reaction, situation)

**Block 2 — Customer Cases**

- Which 2-3 customers/results first come to mind?

- For each case: Who was the person? Where did they start? What changed? Over what period of time?

- Is there a quote or statement from a customer that you will never forget?

**Block 3 — Real-World Objections**

- What are the most common phrases you hear in DMs, comments, or calls when people are hesitant?

- What do most people believe you do — and are wrong about?

- What is the biggest misconception about your product/approach?
