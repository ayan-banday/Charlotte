# Newsletter Becoming the Person Your Goals Belong To — Project Introduction

**One-sentence description:** A newsletter for high-performers who execute hard but hit diminishing returns, teaching them that goals belong to a different identity—not a harder grind.

---

## Why This Project Exists

**Connected to RGS:** Audience expansion. Building a voice in the high-performer space.

**Problem it solves:** High performers chase goals from need—more hours, more hires, more optimization inside the same identity. Each move feels logical but the goal recedes. The constraint is identity, not execution.

**Success looks like:** Readers stop chasing and start becoming. They operate from non-neediness; goals become the natural output of who they are, not a condition for their worth.

---

**Phase:** Ideation.
