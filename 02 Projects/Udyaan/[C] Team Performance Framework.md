# Udyaan Team Performance Framework

## What I am trying to establish

Udyaan should be managed as a connected system of teams and projects.

Each team should have:

1. A clear objective: the part of the Udyaan system it owns.
2. A small weekly scorecard: the measures that show whether that part of the system is healthy.
3. A three-signal daily pulse: whether work is moving, what is stuck, and whether the weekly commitment is still achievable.
4. Evidence behind each reported result: an output, decision, approval, test, review record, or source link.

This is proposed thinking for validation. It is not yet a final target set, an individual-performance system, or a decision to use Notion, Jira, or another tool.

## How the system works

Udyaan programme objectives → team objectives → weekly team measures → daily team pulse → project records and evidence.

Projects supply the facts. Teams own the quality and movement of the system. The programme view shows where leadership attention is needed.

## Working Udyaan objectives

| Objective | What it means |
|---|---|
| Turn real problems into credible solutions | Opportunities are scoped, tested, and progressed through clear decision gates. |
| Make applied learning visible and rigorous | Students and faculty contribute through mapped learning, review, assessment, and evidence. |
| Build an evidence-backed operating system | Every live project has a clear next move, current records, accountable handoffs, and usable data. |
| Convert validated work into venture and impact pathways | Promising solutions have a route to pilot, revenue, incubation, and measurable social or economic value. |

## Daily pulse: the same three signals for every team

| Daily signal | What it answers | How it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Task Completion Rate | Did the team complete the planned tasks for the day? | Completed planned tasks ÷ planned tasks for the day. |
| Work Waiting | What important work is blocked, and for how long? | Number of blocked items plus age of the oldest blocker. |
| Week on Track? | Can the team still achieve its weekly commitments? | Green, Amber, or Red, with one short reason and the help required. |

The daily pulse is not the scorecard. It surfaces movement and blockage early.

## Weekly scorecards

### Project Management

**Objective:** Make every live Udyaan project ready to move, easy to see, and quick to decide.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Project Ready Rate | Active projects with complete scope, current stage, next gate, due date, responsible team, dependencies, risks, and evidence link ÷ active projects. |
| On-Time Gate Rate | Gates completed on or before due date ÷ gates due in the period. |
| First-Time-Right Handoff Rate | Handoffs accepted without return for missing scope, inputs, or evidence ÷ total handoffs. |
| Decision Waiting Time | Median business days from a decision-ready request to a recorded decision. |

### Industry Integration

**Objective:** Convert qualified collaborators into well-scoped delivery opportunities.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Qualified Opportunity Rate | Opportunities with a real problem, relevant partner, decision-maker, potential value, and qualification decision ÷ active opportunities. |
| Problem Brief Acceptance Rate | Problem briefs jointly accepted by Udyaan and the partner without major rework ÷ briefs submitted. |
| Industry-to-Production Handoff Rate | Accepted briefs transferred with user, scope, success measure, resources, timeline, and recipient complete ÷ accepted briefs. |
| Partner Validation Coverage | Active industry projects with a documented partner feedback or validation checkpoint ÷ active industry projects. |

### Academic Integration

**Objective:** Make projects a credible, reviewable learning journey.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Learning-Ready Project Rate | Selected projects with curriculum, attendance, assessment, review, and faculty mapping complete ÷ selected projects. |
| Faculty Coverage Rate | Active projects with an accountable faculty lead and appropriate mentor support ÷ active projects. |
| Student Journey Completion Rate | Selected students who complete onboarding, planned reviews, and assessed outputs ÷ selected students. |
| Academic Review On-Time Rate | Planned academic stakeholder reviews completed on time with recommendations recorded ÷ reviews due. |

### Product and Operations

**Objective:** Move validated briefs through a controlled, first-time-right delivery pipeline.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Build-Ready Brief Rate | Accepted briefs with defined inputs, outputs, quality gates, resources, and release criteria ÷ accepted briefs. |
| On-Time Release Rate | Planned prototypes, events, or operational outputs released by deadline ÷ releases due. |
| First-Time-Right Release Rate | Outputs accepted at quality review without material rework ÷ outputs reviewed. |
| Operational Readiness Rate | Planned work with resources, calendar block, logistics, safety, and quality requirements locked ÷ planned work. |

### Digital Solutions

**Objective:** Make Udyaan data visible, usable, and trustworthy.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Project Visibility Coverage | Active projects shown in the system with stage, next gate, risk, responsible team, and evidence link ÷ active projects. |
| Data Freshness Rate | Active project records updated within the agreed weekly reporting period ÷ active projects. |
| Data Quality Pass Rate | Updates accepted without return for missing fields, invalid values, or broken evidence links ÷ updates reviewed. |
| Platform Adoption Rate | Intended project teams that access or update the shared system during the review cycle ÷ intended project teams. |

### Creatives and Branding

**Objective:** Create a consistent, credible public story that supports launch and partner trust.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Creative Brief Readiness | Requested assets with audience, purpose, message, call to action, evidence source, and deadline complete ÷ active briefs. |
| First-Time-Right Approval Rate | Assets approved without major rework ÷ assets submitted for approval. |
| Launch Asset Readiness | Priority assets approved and deployed by their readiness deadline ÷ priority assets required. |
| Claim Verification Rate | External facts, statistics, and promises with a valid source and approval record ÷ claims reviewed. |

### Business Impact

**Objective:** Turn validated opportunities into measurable venture and impact pathways.

| Weekly measure | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Venture-Ready Opportunity Rate | Priority opportunities with beneficiary, value proposition, revenue or impact pathway, and next decision gate complete ÷ priority opportunities. |
| Validation-to-Pilot Rate | Validated opportunities that progress into a documented pilot ÷ validated opportunities reviewed. |
| Pilot-to-Incubation Rate | Pilots that receive a documented incubation, pivot, pause, or stop decision ÷ pilots reviewed. |
| Revenue and Impact Evidence Coverage | Reported revenue, reach, jobs, savings, or impact figures with calculation, source, period, and confidence rating ÷ figures reported. |

## How weekly updates become team performance

Teams should not calculate scorecards manually. They update simple project facts once a week. The system calculates the measures.

| Register          | One row represents             | What it enables                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project Register  | One live project               | Readiness, stage, responsible team, next gate, risk, and weekly visibility. |
| Gate Register     | One milestone or decision gate | On-time movement and delayed gates.                                         |
| Handoff Register  | One transfer between teams     | First-time-right quality and rework.                                        |
| Decision Register | One decision request           | Decision Waiting Time and overdue decisions.                                |
| Evidence Register | One claim or completed output  | Source coverage, confidence, verification status, and review date.          |

Example:

    18 of 20 active projects have complete records
    → Project Ready Rate = 90%

    8 of 10 gates due this week closed on time
    → On-Time Gate Rate = 80%

    7 of 9 handoffs were accepted first time
    → First-Time-Right Handoff Rate = 78%

## Team health and programme view

Each team should see its four weekly measures separately. A single average can hide a serious problem.

    Project Management
    Project Ready Rate:              90%
    On-Time Gate Rate:               80%
    First-Time-Right Handoff Rate:   78%
    Decision Waiting Time:           4 business days

    Team health: Amber
    Reason: work is moving, but handoffs and decisions are slowing delivery.

The programme view should show:

- Team health: Green, Amber, or Red.
- Number of team measures on target.
- Active projects with no complete weekly update.
- Overdue gates and decisions.
- Blockers requiring cross-team or leadership action.
- Unverified claims or figures.

## Proposed next step

1. Run a two-week baseline pilot.
2. Use the daily pulse and weekly project facts.
3. Do not score teams or set final targets in Week 1.
4. In Week 2, resolve unclear definitions, missing data, and duplicate measures.
5. Set initial targets for one review cycle.
6. Select or configure a tool only after the operating model is validated.

## Validation requested

- Are the seven team scorecards appropriately separated?
- Is the daily pulse light enough to be used consistently?
- Is a two-week baseline pilot the correct next step before tool selection?
