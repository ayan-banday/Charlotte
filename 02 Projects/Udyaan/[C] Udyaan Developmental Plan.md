## 1. Problem Statement

### Who is affected

- **Primary economic actor (buyer):** agri-businesses moving fresh produce farm → mandi / farm → consumer. For this project: **Oyedesi**.
- **Secondary:** farmers supplying that produce (they absorb loss upstream but will not pay for the pack).
- **Tertiary:** end consumers on organic / chemical-free channels who need trust at the pack level.

### Current situation

Fresh crops spoil, bruise, and lose marketability between harvest and sale. Farmers and agri-businesses lose **30–45% of produce value** on that path.

**Priced stake:** on a normal agri-business deal of **₹5 lakh**, about **₹1.6 lakh is lost** (~32%). That money leaves the deal as waste, not as margin.

Existing packs (standard crates, PE liners, ventilated cartons) move produce. They do not reliably:

1. manage ethylene under **ambient** conditions,
2. protect against **compression** in transport and stacking,
3. stay **relatively biodegradable**,
4. carry an **organic / pesticide signal**, or
5. perform inside Oyedesi's **short ambient farm→consumer windows**.

### Why it persists

- Cold chain is expensive and uneven; ambient farm→market is the default for much of the volume Oyedesi moves.
- Packaging is treated as a commodity container, not as a spoilage-control layer.
- Farmers lack willingness (and often cash) to buy premium packs — so solutions aimed at the farmer stall.
- Organic / chemical-free claims are hard to verify at the pack, so trust leaks even when produce quality is fine.
- Agro-waste (e.g. G9 banana pseudostem) is underused as feedstock for the pack itself.

### What changes if it is solved

Oyedesi keeps a larger share of each ₹5L deal (target: cut into the ₹1.6L leakage). Produce arrives firmer, with longer ambient shelf life, in a pack that matches Oyedesi's eco-friendly and organic posture. Farmers still benefit as suppliers (less rejected stock), but **the invoice goes to the agri-business**.

### Solution requirements (must-hit)

| # | Requirement | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manage ethylene — extend shelf life | Farm → mandi, ambient |
| 2 | Protect from compression | Transport + stacking |
| 3 | Relatively biodegradable | Fresh produce use case |
| 4 | Organic / pesticide signal | On-pack trust layer |
| 5 | Works in ambient short farm→consumer windows | Oyedesi ops reality |

### Out of scope (this project block)

Cold-chain redesign. Mandi policy reform. Farmer-direct product sales. Full national loss quantification beyond the ₹5L deal framing.

**Section complete when:** someone outside the team can restate who is hurt, what is lost (₹1.6L / ₹5L), why current packs fail, and that Oyedesi is the buyer.

## 6. Social Impact

### Intended effect

- **Agri-businesses (Oyedesi first):** less produce value destroyed between farm and sale; stronger organic / eco packing story.
- **Farmers in the Oyedesi network:** fewer rejected lots and less silent loss upstream, without asking them to buy the pack.
- **Consumers:** fresher produce and a clearer organic / pesticide trust signal at the pack.
- **Environment:** displace PE liner use with relatively biodegradable materials; valorise G9 banana agro-waste (pseudostem / peel) that is otherwise discarded.
- **Students:** applied R&D, field expert contact, and a priced real problem — not a paper exercise.

### Metrics and baselines

| Impact metric | Baseline | Target (this block / pilot) | How measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value loss on a ₹5L agri-business deal | ~₹1.6L lost (~32%) | Directional reduction on pilot route (exact % TBD after F) | Deal-level waste / rejection logs with Oyedesi |
| Ambient shelf-life (pilot crop G9) | Current unpackaged / PE-liner performance (measure in B) | Measurable extension under ambient short windows | Side-by-side shelf tests |
| Compression damage rate | Current bruising / crush rate on Oyedesi stacks (measure in B) | Lower damage % on pilot packs | Sampled crate inspection |
| Plastic / PE displacement | Current PE liner use on target SKUs | Pilot SKUs on biodegradable alternative | Pack BOM comparison |
| Agro-waste diverted | 0 kg into Udyaan packs | Track kg of G9 waste used in samples | Lab / sample logs |
| Organic trust signal | Claim-only / no on-pack indicator | Indicator mechanism chosen and pilot-feasible | Validation memo (D/F) |
| Student capability | 0 recorded on Udyaan cohort | 10 students contributing to named tasks | Cohort roster + task ownership |

### Sector frame (reference, not the pilot KPI)

National post-harvest food loss ~₹1.53 lakh crore/year (NABCONS 2022). Udyaan does not claim national impact in this block. The **pilot baseline is the ₹5L deal / Oyedesi route.**
