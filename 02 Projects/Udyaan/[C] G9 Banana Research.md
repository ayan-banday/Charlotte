# G9 Banana Research

**Pilot crop for Udyaan packaging.** G9 = Grand Naine, India's dominant export-grade Cavendish cultivar.

**Links:** [[C] Udyaan Project Schematics]] · [[C] Udyaan Packaging POA]] · [[C] Udyaan Problem Statement]]

## Why G9

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| Market | India produces ~30% of global bananas; G9 is the standard commercial cultivar |
| Loss severity | Post-harvest losses commonly cited at **30–40%** for banana (vs 6–15% national fruit average) |
| Climacteric | High ethylene producer — ripens fast after harvest; packaging must manage ethylene, not block it blindly |
| Udyaan fit | Tests all 4 solution layers: ethylene, compression, biodegradability, organic/pesticide signal |
| Export path | G9 already has established pack specs (CFB boxes, reefer) — Udyaan can improve the farm→mandi leg |

## G9 Post-Harvest Profile

**Scientific name:** *Musa paradisiaca* L. cv. Grand Naine (G9)

**Harvest stage:** 75–80% maturity (3/4 full, edges rounded, dark green) for export; earlier for local mandi depending on transit days

**Ripening trigger:** Ethylene-dependent climacteric fruit. Ripening accelerates sharply at climacteric peak.

**Shelf life (ambient, no treatment):** ~9–10 days unpackaged (IGKV Raipur study)

**Shelf life (with packaging):**
- LDPE bags: up to **18.67 days** (best ambient performer in comparative study)
- LDPE 200 gauge + CaCl₂ dip: maintains TSS, ascorbic acid, sensory quality
- Active packaging (ethylene scrubber + silica gel in LDPE): up to **26 days**; spoilage ~45% vs higher in control at 24 days
- Cold chain (13.2–13.5°C, 85–90% RH): green life up to **45 days** (export standard)

**Primary loss drivers for G9:**
1. Rapid ethylene-driven ripening
2. Mechanical damage (bruising, crown damage) during harvest + stacking
3. Crown rot (fungal — needs field washing in alum water, fungicide at crown)
4. Physiological weight loss (moisture escape)
5. Artificial ripening abuse (calcium carbide — health hazard, erodes organic trust)

## Packaging Benchmarks (Current Industry)

| Material | Gauge / spec | Ventilation | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LDPE bags | 200 gauge | standard | Best chemical + sensory quality with CaCl₂ dip | 2025 IGKV |
| LDPE bags | — | — | 18.67 days shelf life, lowest weight loss | Manikpuri 2025 |
| HDPE bags | — | — | Good; secondary to LDPE | Manikpuri 2025 |
| LDPE | 300 gauge | **1% vents** | Lowest spoilage, best firmness at ambient | IJCMAS 2017 |
| CFB boxes | 13.5 kg or 18 kg | bottom-vented | Export standard; stacking without crush | Export guide |
| PP/LDPE + KMnO₄ sachet (5g) | active pack | — | Ethylene scrubbing extends life | AP study |
| PP/LDPE + silica gel (2g) | active pack | — | Moisture control | AP study |

**Key finding:** 300 gauge LDPE with **1% ventilation** = best ambient loss minimization for Grand Naine specifically.

## Ethylene Requirements (G9-Specific)

G9 produces and responds to ethylene aggressively. Udyaan packaging must:

- **Not trap ethylene** in closed bags without ventilation (accelerates ripening)
- **Regulate** via controlled permeability OR active scavenging (KMnO₄ ethylene absorbers)
- **1% vent area** on polyethylene = validated sweet spot for Grand Naine at ambient
- For mandi transit (no cold chain): modified atmosphere via LDPE + vents + optional scrubber sachet

---

## Compression Requirements (G9-Specific)

- Bananas bruise easily — finger pressure causes latent damage
- Export uses **telescopic CFB boxes** for stackability without direct fruit-on-fruit pressure
- Farm→mandi leg often uses open crates or gunny — high compression loss
- Udyaan needs: rigid outer structure + inner cushioning/separation OR individual fruit protection

---

## Biodegradability (G9 Context)

- Current best performers = LDPE/HDPE (not biodegradable)
- Udyaan differentiator: match LDPE performance on ethylene + moisture with **biodegradable substrate**
- Candidates to research: PLA, starch-based films, banana fiber pulp trays (ironic circularity), chitosan coatings (also antimicrobial)
- Chitosan coating showed best shelf-life extension in active packaging study — dual function

---

## Organic / Pesticide Signal (G9 Context)

- G9 commonly treated with fungicides at crown post-harvest (export standard)
- Calcium carbide ripening = widespread in India, illegal, undermines organic claim
- Organic G9 buyers need on-pack verification that:
  - No carbide ripening occurred
  - Pesticide residue within organic limits
- Packaging indicator must survive humid banana storage conditions
