# Udyaan Developmental Plan

Problem → requirements → timelines → plan of action. Ships the R&D deliverable set for Oyedesi.

## 1. Problem Statement

Farmers and agri-businesses lose **30–45% of produce value** because fresh crops spoil between farm and market.

**Priced stake:** on a normal agri-business deal of **₹5 lakh**, that is about **₹1.6 lakh lost** (~32%).

**Who pays:** farmers will not buy this. The buyer is the **agri-business** — **Oyedesi**. They take the loss on the deal, so they own the incentive to fund the fix.

**Wedge:** packaging on the farm → mandi / farm → consumer path. Ambient conditions. No cold-chain dependency for the first version.

---

## 2. Solution Requirements

What the pack must do for Oyedesi. Must-hit before anything else ships.

### Core objectives

| # | Requirement | Scope | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Manage ethylene** | Farm → mandi, ambient | Extends shelf life; slows premature ripening/spoilage without cold chain |
| 2 | **Protect from compression** | Transport + stacking | Cuts bruising/crushing losses in stacked crates and rough handling |
| 3 | **Relatively biodegradable** | Fresh produce use case | Matches Oyedesi eco-friendly packing posture; replaces / supplements PE liners |
| 4 | **Organic / pesticide signal** | On-pack trust layer | Supports Oyedesi chemical-free / organic positioning; buyer can read status at pack level |
| 5 | **Works in ambient short farm→consumer windows** | Oyedesi ops reality | Their model harvests early morning and moves fast to consumer — pack must perform in short, ambient windows |

### Success criteria (project done when)

- [ ] Problem is priced and owned: ₹1.6L / ₹5L deal framing accepted by Oyedesi stakeholders
- [ ] Ethylene management demonstrated for pilot crop under ambient farm→mandi conditions
- [ ] Compression protection holds under realistic stacking / transport loads
- [ ] Pack is relatively biodegradable on a defined compost / soil timeline (target TBD after materials draft)
- [ ] Organic / pesticide indicator mechanism chosen and feasible for pilot batch
- [ ] Pack performance validated against Oyedesi's short ambient farm→consumer window
- [ ] Unit economics: pack cost < value of loss prevented for the agri-business deal

### Explicitly out of scope for this plan

Cold-chain redesign. Mandi policy. Farmer-direct sales. Full national loss quantification beyond the ₹5L deal framing.

## 4. Plan of Action

Deliverable set for this developmental block: **G9 research → materials ratios → department visits → budget lines → solution hypothesis.**

### 4.1 G9 banana research deep dive (~2 days)

**Goal:** understand the pilot crop well enough to pick materials and talk to experts without hand-waving.

**Cover:**

- G9 (Grand Naine / Cavendish) biology relevant to packaging: ethylene sensitivity, ripening stages, skin strength, shelf life under ambient
- Post-harvest chain for G9 in India: farm → packhouse → transport → mandi / consumer (map where the ₹1.6L leakage hits hardest)
- Waste stream: pseudostem, peel, leaf — volumes and cellulose / pectin potential
- Current packaging norms: ventilated cartons, PE liners, foam dividers, ethylene absorbers (KMnO₄ sachets) — what we replace or improve
- Fit to Oyedesi: ambient short windows vs longer mandi / export legs — which path is v1

**Output:** research notes (promote into schematics when stable). Crop choice locked for pilot: **G9**.

### 4.2 Materials plan — ratios and proportions (~1 day draft)

**Goal:** draft which inputs and in what proportions, before expert validation.

**Working direction (literature baseline — revise after Jain visits):**

| Component | Role | Working range to research |
|---|---|---|
| Banana pseudostem cellulose / pectin | Reinforcement / film former | Primary feedstock from G9 waste |
| Starch or CMC matrix | Film body | Balance strength vs flexibility |
| Glycerol (or equivalent plasticizer) | Flexibility | Prevent brittle failure in stacking |
| Optional additives | Ethylene control / indicator | Absorbers, scavengers, or chemical indicator layer |

**Output:** one-page materials draft with proposed % ranges and open questions for Visit 1 / Visit 2.

### 4.3 Department visits — Jain University

**Campus:** Jain Global Campus, NH-209, Jakkasandra Post, Kanakapura Taluk (~45 km from Bangalore; ~50–55 km from central city). Plan travel time + reimbursement accordingly.

| Visit | Department / centre | Who they are for | Agenda |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visit 1** | **Department of Chemistry & Biochemistry** (School of Sciences) | Biochemists | Can we extract viable biopolymers from G9 pseudostem / peel? Food-contact safety? Biodegradation profile? Organic / pesticide indicator chemistry — feasible? |
| **Visit 2** | **Centre for Nano & Material Sciences (CNMS)** | Material scientists | Film mechanical strength under compression / stacking. Controlled ethylene permeability under ambient. Characterization path (tensile, barrier, FTIR/SEM if available). Proportions that survive Oyedesi short-window ops. |

**Cadence:** ~1 visit per day. Do not collapse both into one meeting — different labs, different questions.

**Bring to each visit:** problem priced (₹1.6L / ₹5L), the five solution requirements, G9 research notes, materials draft.

### 4.5 Solution hypothesis (deliverable)

**Status:** draft direction — lock after Visits 1 and 2. Not the pitch yet.

**Working hypothesis:**

A biodegradable packaging system derived from **G9 banana agro-waste** (pseudostem cellulose and/or pectin, with a starch/CMC matrix and plasticizer at defined proportions) can cut Oyedesi's post-harvest loss on ambient farm→consumer / farm→mandi legs by managing ethylene and protecting against compression, while meeting Oyedesi's biodegradable and organic-signal requirements — at a unit cost below the ₹1.6L-per-₹5L-deal loss it prevents.

**Deliverable checklist:**

- [ ] Hypothesis statement in one paragraph (final wording after visits)
- [ ] Pilot crop: G9
- [ ] Materials ratios (v1 numbers from experts)
- [ ] Which requirements are validated vs still open
- [ ] Recommended next build step (lab sample / supplier scan / pilot batch)
