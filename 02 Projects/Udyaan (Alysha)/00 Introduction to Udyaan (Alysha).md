# Udyaan (Alysha)

Smart biodegradable packaging for fresh produce — cuts post-harvest loss, protects through transport, and signals organic/pesticide status at the pack level.

**Connected to RGS:** Q3 (₹1L contract, price one problem, 10 students) · Q4 (₹10L Udyaan revenue, 3 premium pitches)

## Why This Exists

Farmers and agri-businesses lose 30–45% of produce value (Ash's field estimate; national data varies by stage — see schematics) because fresh crops spoil between farm and market. Packaging, transport, grading, and shelf life are the leak points. Udyaan targets the packaging layer as the wedge.
