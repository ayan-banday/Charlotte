# Brain Dump for Venture-Scale Opportunity

## Queue import — August 30

### VC opportunity framing

- A venture case needs an opportunity large enough to matter and a structural change that creates room for a new company.
- A useful pitch starts with the user’s real story, the monetary value of the loss, the initial wedge, and how the wedge compounds into a much larger market.
- Market size alone is not the case. The project needs a nonlinear mechanism, a bridge to expansion, and a disproof condition.

### Learning method

- Pause a useful source, apply each point to this project, then continue. This produces better encoding than passive watching.
- Passive research is a known failure path. Every reading or video should leave one written claim, evidence tag, and next test.

### Open questions

- What is the exact v1 decision scene?
- Which economic event makes proposal qualification or scope-exception review valuable enough to buy?
- What evidence would make the current candidate a Pass rather than a Probe?
