# Potential Collaborator Alignment

**Participants:** Ayan Banday and Shlok Goel  
**Project:** Venture-Scale Opportunity  
**Status:** Discussion draft for mutual alignment  
**Date:** September 14, 2026

## Purpose

This document records the current understanding between Ayan and Shlok as they explore whether they should work together on the project. It is intended to make expectations, learning goals, responsibilities, and future decisions clear before either person makes a deeper commitment.

It is a working document, not an employment agreement, equity agreement, partnership deed, or legal commitment. Any future equity, compensation, company formation, intellectual-property, or governance terms must be discussed separately and documented formally if both parties choose to proceed.

## Project Context

The project is in an early discovery phase. Its purpose is to test whether there is a significant, recurring business problem that can support a venture-scale company.

The current exploration concerns decision infrastructure for execution-heavy B2B businesses. The working hypothesis is that companies lose revenue, margin, or delivery quality when important customer, sales, marketing, onboarding, and delivery decisions are made from fragmented information rather than reliable evidence.

No product, customer segment, technical build, funding outcome, or company structure is committed yet. The immediate work is to understand the problem through research and conversations with potential customers and operators, then decide whether a narrow, valuable workflow exists.

## Why We Are Exploring This Collaboration

Ayan is looking for a potential collaborator who can grow into meaningful technical and commercial ownership if the opportunity proves real and the working relationship proves strong.

Shlok is looking for practical experience that helps him become a stronger candidate for a future founder's-office role in a technology company. He wants to deepen his technical ability while learning how early-stage companies approach sales, marketing, customer conversations, networks, fundraising, and cross-functional decisions.

The collaboration is therefore an exploration of two questions:

1. Can the project become a business worth building?
2. Can Ayan and Shlok work together in a way that creates value for the business and meaningful growth for both people?

## Shlok's Intended Outcomes

Through this collaboration, Shlok intends to develop:

- Stronger judgment about how an early-stage company identifies and validates a problem.
- Exposure to customer discovery, venture fundraising, founder-led sales and marketing, and business operations.
- A better understanding of how technical work connects to product, customer value, go-to-market strategy, and company economics.
- Experience that is more substantive and differentiated than a routine internship.
- A clear, respectful process for discussing future ownership if his contribution and the project justify it.

Shlok remains responsible for his academic work and for developing his technical skills independently. This collaboration is intended to complement those priorities, not replace them.

## Ayan's Intended Outcomes

Ayan intends to assess whether Shlok can become a strong long-term collaborator by observing his initiative, judgment, learning speed, reliability, technical capability, and ability to think beyond a narrow task.

If the collaboration develops well, Ayan expects Shlok to contribute to:

- Understanding the customer problem and the workflow worth solving.
- Connecting technical choices to business value and customer outcomes.
- Participating in discussions about product, go-to-market, and operating decisions.
- Supporting technical research, prototypes, integrations, or product development when the evidence supports that work.
- Building the habits of a founder-level collaborator: raising questions, proposing solutions, taking ownership, and participating honestly in difficult decisions.

## Initial Collaboration Model

### Current phase

The current phase is discovery, not product development. The priority is evidence: customer conversations, problem reconstruction, market research, and a decision about whether to pursue, probe further, or stop.

### Initial activity

Over the next two to four weeks, the primary activity will be more frequent working conversations between Ayan and Shlok. These conversations should help Shlok understand the project, test how the two of you think together, and identify a useful contribution area.

The purpose is not to create busywork. Any work assigned should connect to a real learning objective or a real project decision.

### Time expectation

The collaboration is expected to be mentally demanding at times, but not a full-time commitment. The exact rhythm will depend on the project stage, Shlok's academic workload, and the value of the work being done.

### Communication rhythm

Ayan and Shlok will create a shared Discord channel. It will be the main place to keep each other informed about:

- Current work and learning.
- Relevant customer, market, or technical findings.
- Questions, decisions, and blockers.
- Commitments made during calls and their status.

Calls will be used for discussion, learning, decisions, and work that benefits from real-time thinking. The exact call cadence will be agreed through use rather than fixed prematurely.

## Principles for Future Ownership Discussions

No equity range, percentage, or cap is agreed at this stage.

If the collaboration continues beyond the review period, Ayan and Shlok will discuss ownership using evidence rather than assumptions. The discussion should consider:

- The significance and consistency of each person's contribution.
- The responsibilities each person has actually taken on.
- Technical, commercial, customer, and operating value created.
- Time commitment, opportunity cost, and risk assumed.
- The work still required to build the company.
- Whether both people want a long-term founder-level relationship.

Any future equity proposal should also address vesting, contribution milestones, decision-making rights, and what happens if either person chooses to leave. Shlok will research founder-equity approaches and prepare a proposal or discussion brief as part of his learning process.

## Review Period and Decision

The trial period has not yet been decided. Ayan and Shlok will agree whether the first formal review occurs after **60 days or 90 days**.

At that review, the two of you will assess:

| Area | Question to answer |
| --- | --- |
| Project evidence | Is there a specific problem, customer, and workflow worth pursuing further? |
| Working relationship | Do we communicate well, challenge each other constructively, and follow through? |
| Shlok's contribution | Has Shlok shown initiative, learning, reliability, and useful founder-level potential? |
| Mutual fit | Do both people want to continue working together more deeply? |
| Next commitment | Should we continue, revise the arrangement, pause, or separate? |

The review may lead to continued exploration, a revised working model, a formal co-founder discussion, or an amicable decision not to continue. None of those outcomes is predetermined.
