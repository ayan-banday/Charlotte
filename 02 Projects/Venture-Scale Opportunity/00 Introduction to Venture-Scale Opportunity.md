# Venture-Scale Opportunity

Develop and test a venture-scale software opportunity that can clear the RGS wedge and venture-readiness barriers.

**Status:** Discovery — Probe

## Current Status

September is for venture literacy and market research, not product build. The working concept is decision infrastructure for founder-led service firms: cited, importance-ranked recommendations for proposal qualification and scope-exception review, with mandatory escalation when confidence is low.

The candidate ICP is 20–75 person performance-marketing agencies using HubSpot. It is a hypothesis, not a committed direction. No customer conversations, validated workflow, economic case, or committed wedge exists yet.

## September Outcome

By September 30, produce a market-research document with:

- A problem statement.
- Sourced market size in dollars.
- Ten competitors.
- A venture-scale verdict: Pursue, Probe, or Pass.
- A wedge angle and explicit disproof condition.
- Evidence labelled as Fact, Inference, Hypothesis, or Story.

The same work supports the 15–20 minute professor presentation on VC rounds, dilution, category ownership, and how billion-dollar companies are built.

## What Must Become True

- One narrow ICP, recurring trigger, and bounded workflow are selected.
- The workflow connects to a revenue, margin, or risk event, rather than only founder time savings.
- The market-size and expansion case has named bridges and evidence.
- Customer reconstruction exposes real past behavior and usable artifacts.
- The proposed system beats a generic-AI baseline on a declared metric and guardrails.
