# Campaign Strategy Doc — ICS Event Announcement Sequence

---

## Product

**Name:** Free 1:1 Call with Ash **Price:** Free. Cost is accountability: staying in the group chat and reporting back on actions taken. **Unique Mechanism:** A single focused conversation that diagnoses which of three root causes is keeping the student stuck, and gives them one concrete next move. **Guarantee:** None needed. Zero financial risk. The only thing they lose is the time on the call.

---

## Entry Point

**Format:** Discord announcements (not email) **Channels:** In-house announcements + all member channels **What they already know:** They know Ash. They've likely received help from him directly or seen him active in the community. Trust is pre-loaded. **What's being offered:** An event on May 31st. Not a webinar. An event. **Delivered after signup:** They tag the assistant in #off-topic. The assistant reaches out, collects their tag, adds them to the list. Every subsequent announcement tags them directly.

---

## Customer Avatar

**Who:** ICS students who have been in the program for months and are still at the same stage. They are not lazy. They are working, often for hours. But nothing is moving.

**Has already tried:**

- Rebuilding their SIR schedule from scratch
- Reorganizing mindmaps
- Switching apps and task managers
- Reading articles about learning instead of applying
- Waiting to feel ready before starting

**Why it didn't work:** None of these address the actual mechanism. They're behavioral fixes for a structural problem.

**Awareness Level at entry:** Level 3 to 4. They know they have a problem. Most have tried solutions. Some know of Ash and his approach already.

**Strongest Life's 8 Triggers:**

- 3: Freedom from fear (fear of falling further behind, fear of wasting more months)
- 6: Superiority (the gap between how hard they feel they're working and what peers seem to be producing)
- 8: Social recognition (shame of still being at the same stage, feeling like they're the only one)

**Surface Desire:** To stop procrastinating and actually make progress in ICS. **Real Desire:** To feel like the effort they're putting in is actually moving something. **Deep Desire:** To not feel like they're falling behind or doing it wrong while everyone else figures it out.

## Beliefs

**Beliefs present at entry (Problematic):**

- "This is too simple a problem. I should have solved this by now."
- "I've understood the problem before and still didn't change. Understanding it again won't help."
- "I don't have time for something that might not be specifically for me."
- "I'm just not disciplined enough."
- "I need to be in the right state before I can actually learn."

**Beliefs that must be present at signup (Required):**

- "This is not a discipline problem. The mechanism is specific and I haven't actually understood mine yet."
- "Ash has seen enough versions of this to know which one is mine."
- "This event will give me something concrete. Not general advice."
- "Missing this would mean staying stuck for another month."

**Delta the sequence must deliver:**

- From: "I know I'm procrastinating, another thing about motivation won't help" → To: "This is naming the exact thing I'm doing. I need to be in that room."

## Top Objections (soft to hard)

1. "This is too simple a problem. I should have already solved it." (shame-based dismissal)
2. "I've tried understanding this before. I still didn't change." (skepticism about insight without action)
3. "I don't have time for something that might not be for me." (relevance uncertainty)

## Funnel Congruence Check

**Event Core Claim:** You're not procrastinating. You're working on the wrong thing. And which specific wrong thing it is can be identified and fixed.

**Announcement → Event Bridge:** Announcements name the problems precisely enough that the student recognizes their own flavor. The event is framed as the place to find out which one is theirs and what to do about it.

**Event → Call Bridge:** The event ends with a pitch for the 1:1 call. The call is where the diagnosis gets specific to them personally.

## Announcement 1 — Detailed Brief

**Approach:** Lead with problems. No credentials. No setup. Name the three patterns with enough specificity that each reader sees themselves. Then land the event.

**Entry framing:** Do not name procrastination upfront. Name the actual behaviors. Land "this has a name" at the end.

**Three patterns to name:**

- Your nervous system is already in a stress response before you open a book. (Doom-scrolling, bad sleep, unresolved weight from earlier in the day. You sit down and feel torn in two.)
- You're working but nothing is moving because your tasks have no success state. "Read two chapters" can be done forever. You never know if it worked. (Thomsen reorganized mindmaps for three hours. Kafka waited to recover a feeling. RyeBread built a tracking template for six weeks. All working. None of it a rep.)
- You're producing toward a goal you can't picture anymore. The course became the point. The original reason got quiet.

**Objection to neutralize in Announcement 1:** "This is too simple." → Counter by naming the specific flavor, not the category. The specificity IS the credibility.

**CTA:** If any of this sounds like you, tag [assistant name] in #off-topic. He'll reach out. You'll be on the list for the event on May 31st.

**What this announcement does NOT do:**

- Does not explain the solutions
- Does not pitch the 1:1 call
- Does not mention Ash's credentials or role
- Does not use the word procrastination as the lead

## Voice Notes

- Ash's real language from the webinar script and brain dump takes priority. Verbatim where possible.
- Short paragraphs. Line breaks do the work in Discord.
- Energy is warm but direct. Not motivational-speaker. Not academic. Peer who has seen this a hundred times.
- No em dashes. No bullet-pointed lists of tips. This is not advice. It is recognition.
