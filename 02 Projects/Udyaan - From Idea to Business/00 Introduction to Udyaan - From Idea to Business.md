# Udyaan - From Idea to Business

Practical entrepreneurship course for Udyaan students who need to become business-capable by building, selling, and adapting a real business opportunity.

**Parent context:** Udyaan

**Status:** Course architecture and visual blueprint proposal created; curriculum build not yet started.

**Primary deliverable:** [[Udyaan From Idea to Business - Course Blueprint Proposal.pdf]]

## Current Status

The course is designed as a three-phase experiential journey. Students enter with an interest in entrepreneurship and may have a business idea, but they do not yet understand how an idea becomes a business.

The course uses a recurring story about a non-human adventurer mascot who learns to:

1. Map the territory and find a valuable customer problem.
2. Forge an offer designed for that problem.
3. Enter the market, test demand, build, learn, and iterate.

The course is hybrid: written lessons, short Loom videos, attached GPT practice, real-world assignments, artifact submission, and AI/human feedback.

## Learner Transformation

**Before:** “I have an idea, but I do not know how to turn it into a business.”

**After:** The student can identify a customer problem, explain a value proposition, create an offer, test demand, sell, build a first version, and adapt based on evidence.

## Three-Phase Structure

### Phase 1 - Map the Territory

Understand business, choose a market segment, find the customer problem, and measure its value.

### Phase 2 - Forge the Weapon

Define the customer result, shape the value proposition, create the offer, and prepare sales material.

### Phase 3 - Enter the Battle

Find prospects, conduct outreach, test payment or commitment, deliver the smallest useful version, and iterate.

## What Every Student Should Leave With

- A defined market territory and customer group
- A problem statement supported by customer evidence
- An estimate of the problem's urgency and economic value
- A concrete offer with customer, result, scope, delivery method, and price logic
- Basic copywriting and outreach material
- Evidence from a demand test or pre-sale attempt
- A first delivery, prototype, service, or workflow
- A revised offer and evidence-based next-build plan

The course does not promise that every idea will succeed. It promises that every student will gain real-world business experience and learn how to navigate uncertainty through evidence, action, and iteration.
