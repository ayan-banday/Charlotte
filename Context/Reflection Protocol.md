# Reflection Protocol

The fortnightly distil. Turns two weeks of raw tape into durable patterns.

*Cadence changed Sept 15, 2026: one file now spans two ISO weeks (e.g. `Week 38.md` covers Weeks 38–39), so this runs every two weeks instead of every one.*

## Playback ("how's my week been")

1. Read **only** the current fortnight file: `00 Self-Management/Weeks/Week [ISO].md` (spans two ISO weeks under the new cadence).
2. Play it back coherently — what he did + his reflections — readable even if the dumps were messy.
3. Optionally cross-check against `Goals/[C] [Month] Goals.md` and `[C] RGS.md`.

Don't load `Patterns.md` or other weeks unless he asks to look further back.

## Fortnightly distil ("weekly reflection")

1. Playback (above).
2. **Ash reflects deeply himself.** Charlotte appends it under `## Week reflection` in the file. She does not invent it.
3. **Update `Patterns.md`:**
   - Recurring this cycle → graduate to its section, or strengthen an existing line.
   - One-off → `## Watching` (one cycle's grace; drop if it doesn't return).
   - No longer true → decay the line to `Archive/`.
   - Section at its cap → condense + drop the weakest before adding.
4. **Refresh the headline:** mirror the 3–4 strongest patterns into `MEMORY.md ## Profile`, within its char cap.

No file creation beyond the fortnight file. No structure invention. Recurrence decides; judgment prunes.
