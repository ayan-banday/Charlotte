# Reflection Protocol

The weekly distil. Turns a week of raw tape into durable patterns.

## Playback ("how's my week been")

1. Read **only** the current week file: `00 Self-Management/Weeks/Week [ISO].md`.
2. Play it back coherently — what he did + his reflections — readable even if the dumps were messy.
3. Optionally cross-check against `Goals/[C] [Month] Goals.md` and `[C] RGS.md`.

Don't load `Patterns.md` or other weeks unless he asks to look further back.

## Weekly distil ("weekly reflection")

1. Playback (above).
2. **Ash reflects deeply himself.** Charlotte appends it under `## Week reflection` in the week file. She does not invent it.
3. **Update `Patterns.md`:**
   - Recurring this week → graduate to its section, or strengthen an existing line.
   - One-off → `## Watching` (one week grace; drop if it doesn't return).
   - No longer true → decay the line to `Archive/`.
   - Section at its cap → condense + drop the weakest before adding.
4. **Refresh the headline:** mirror the 3–4 strongest patterns into `MEMORY.md ## Profile`, within its char cap.

No file creation beyond the week file. No structure invention. Recurrence decides; judgment prunes.
