**The barrier:** Transitioning to studying feels like "a new task getting started." The friction of initiation hits again, even though the preparation is done.

## Learning Loop: Mentor + Real-World + First Principles + Practice

*What it produces:* Fast, integrated learning. Theory + application in one cycle.

**Your webinar funnel learning process:**

1. **Get help from mentor.** Direct guidance, avoid dead-ends.
2. **Take real-world examples.** Study what works in the wild.
3. **Copy them.** Reverse-engineer the structure, not just the idea.
4. **Make them better.** Iterate on what you copied. Find gaps.
5. **Understand first principles.** Why does this work? What's the core mechanic?
6. **Try your own thing.** Apply the principles in your own way.
7. **Practice in real world.** Record actual webinar with actual people. Not simulation—real stakes.

**Why this works:** You're not stuck in theory. You're not copying blindly. You're cycling through mentor guidance, real examples, first principles, and live practice. Each loop tightens understanding.

**Next:** Record actual webinar with real people. That's where the learning becomes permanent.

That's Phase 1. Fear so specific it functions like a hand on your back every single morning, whether you feel like it or not.

I got things wrong. Thought engineering was the way to build things. It wasn't the right fit. I kept testing until I found what actually fit: figuring out what people want and what stops them from getting it. The gap between those two things. That's business. That's what I love.

That's Phase 2. The work stops being the price you pay for not becoming your nightmare. It becomes the thing itself.

I haven't hit Phase 3 yet. Purpose. The calling beyond yourself. But I can already feel the difference between fear as fuel and love as fuel. Love burns longer. It's cleaner. You stop checking whether you feel motivated and just go.

**The three phases:**

**Phase 1, Pain.** The image of the life you won't accept keeps you moving. You don't need to love it yet. You just need the nightmare to be vivid enough.

**Phase 2, Love.** You find what you actually want to fight for. The work starts pulling instead of pushing.

**Phase 3, Purpose.** You realize this was never just about you. You work more, not less.
